About

A calm way to keep track of your baby's day.

Babino puts the things you log most on one screen. Start a breastfeed, a bottle or a nap with a single tap, even at 3 a.m. and with one hand. Your logs are saved on your iPhone and work fully offline, with no account to create. Over time Babino turns them into simple charts and reminders, so you can see how your baby is doing and answer the pediatrician's questions at a glance.

FeedingBreastfeeding with a timer for each side, bottles, pumping and first meals. See at a glance when the last feed was.
SleepTime naps and nights, follow a live timer on the Lock Screen, and get age-based sleep suggestions.
DiapersLog diapers in a tap and keep an eye on daily counts.
Growth & progressRecord weight, height and head size, and follow your baby's progress on clear charts.
Illness & medicineTemperatures, symptoms and medicines with doses, grouped into illness episodes you can show your doctor.
Built for twinsTrack several babies side by side, each with their own color. Pick a baby in one tap when you log.
Ask the pediatricianAn AI assistant answers everyday baby questions. Your baby's name, photos and exact dates are never sent.
Private by designNo account, no ads, no tracking. Your baby's logs stay on your phone, and you can export them anytime.

Free to start. The everyday logs, the last 7 days of history and temperature, medicine and symptom logging are free. Babino Pro adds more babies, all charts and history, export, widgets and more.
