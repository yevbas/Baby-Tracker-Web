Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026 · Applies to the Babino iOS app

Yevhen Basistyi ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and your baby's. This Privacy Policy explains how Baby Tracker - Babino (the "App") handles information. In short: your baby's logs stay on your phone, there is no account, no ads and no tracking, and the only time information about your baby leaves your device is when you use the optional chat.

01

Information stored on your device

Everything you log in the App stays on your iPhone. This includes your baby's name, birth date, sex and photo, feeds, sleep, diapers, growth, mood, temperatures, symptoms, medicines, illness episodes, notes, reminders and your chats with the assistant.

This data is not synced to iCloud and is not sent to us. Widgets and Lock Screen timers read it from your device only. It is removed when you delete the App. You can export it from the App's settings, and you decide where an exported file goes.

02

Your baby's health data

Temperatures, symptoms, medicines and illnesses are health information about your child. We treat it as sensitive, special-category data. It is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared, except in the summary you can choose to send with the chat, described below.

03

Ask the pediatrician (AI chat)

The chat is optional. When you send a question, the App sends your question and the conversation so far to our server function (hosted on Google Firebase), which passes it to OpenAI to write an answer. Before it leaves your device, the App removes names, email addresses, phone numbers and links it recognises, and the server removes them again.

If you allow it, a short de-identified summary of your baby's logs goes along so the answers fit your child: age, sex, whether your baby was born early, growth measurements, sleep and feeding averages, the sleep schedule, foods tried, diaper counts, current symptoms and temperature, medicines given and past illnesses, with times described as "days ago" rather than dates. Without your permission, only a rough age range is sent. You can switch this on or off at any time from the menu in the chat.

Never sent: your baby's name, birth date, photo, your notes, or exact dates.

Our server does not store your chats. Requests to OpenAI are made with storage turned off and without a user identifier; OpenAI may keep request data for a limited time for abuse and safety monitoring under its privacy policy, and does not use it to train its models. Your chat history is saved only on your phone, and you can delete any conversation.

04

Anonymous app identifier

The App creates a silent, anonymous account with Firebase Authentication (Google). It has no email, name or password and only gives your install a random identifier. Together with Firebase App Check, which uses Apple's App Attest to confirm requests come from the genuine App, it protects the chat service from abuse. Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy information.

05

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. To manage your subscription status, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives the anonymous app identifier and purchase information from Apple. The App also lets RevenueCat collect Apple's Search Ads attribution token, which tells us whether an install came from an App Store ad; it contains no personal information. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its own privacy policy.

06

Permissions

  • Notifications — for the reminders you set, such as naps, bedtime, feeds or medicines. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Photos — only if you choose a photo for your baby's profile, using Apple's photo picker. The App receives only the photo you pick, not your library, and the photo stays on your device.

You can change these at any time in iOS Settings. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or Apple Health.

07

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Babino contains no advertising and no analytics SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

08

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data. The only data leaving your device is what is described above: chat messages and, if you allow it, the de-identified summary (Google Firebase and OpenAI), the anonymous app identifier (Google Firebase and RevenueCat), and subscription information (Apple and RevenueCat). These providers act on our behalf and may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

09

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR (European Union) or the CCPA (California) give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion. Because your logs and chats are kept on your device, you can see, change, export or delete them in the App, or delete everything by removing the App. Sending a health summary with the chat is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in the chat menu. For questions about the anonymous identifier or subscription data, contact us at the address below. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

10

Children's privacy

Babino is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children to use, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Information about your baby is entered by you, stays on your device and is handled as described in this policy.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

12
